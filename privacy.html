3. Who else processes your data

We only share your data with the providers we need to run this website:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) – delivers the contact form to our email inbox.
  • Google – Gmail (our inbox), Google Fonts (loads the site's typefaces, which sends your IP address to Google) and, only with your consent, Google Analytics.
  • Meta Platforms – only with your consent, the Meta Pixel.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) – hosts this website and may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

Some of these providers are located in or process data in the United States or other countries that may not offer a level of protection equivalent to Argentine law. By using the contact form you consent to this international transfer, which is made only to deliver the service described here. We may also disclose data when required by law or by a competent authority.

5. Your rights

You can, free of charge:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data (“right to erasure” or supresión).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • If the GDPR applies to you: object to or restrict processing, and receive your data in a portable format.

Write to caniggiaagency@gmail.com from the email address you used to contact us, or use the form below. We will answer access requests within 10 calendar days and rectification or deletion requests within 5 working days, as required by Law 25,326.

6. Delete my data

Data stored in this browser

Deletes your cookie choice and any Google Analytics or Meta cookies set by this website on this device. The cookie banner will appear again.

Messages you sent us

Ask us to delete everything you sent through the contact form (including the copy in our email inbox). Use the same email address you used to contact us so we can find your messages.

7. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and does not store your data on its own servers. Messages are kept in a password-protected email account that only authorized CFA staff can access.
