About Us

More than an agency, a career partner

Our Story

Caniggia Football Agency was born from a simple idea: players deserve the guidance of people who truly understand the game. Led by Claudio Caniggia and a team of experienced professionals, CFA combines first-hand knowledge of elite football with a modern, transparent approach to player representation.

Our Mission

To help every player reach their full potential, on and off the pitch, by building long-term careers and connecting talent with the right clubs worldwide.

Our Values

  • TrustHonest, long-term relationships with players, families and clubs.
  • ProfessionalismRigorous work in every negotiation and every decision.
  • ExcellencePremium standards inspired by the highest level of the game.
  • CommitmentWe stay by our players' side at every stage of their careers.

The Face of CFA

Claudio Caniggia

One of the most electrifying forwards of his generation, Claudio Caniggia played at the highest level in Argentina and Europe and represented his country at three FIFA World Cups.

  1. River PlateDebut and rise in Argentine football.
  2. Serie AHellas Verona, Atalanta and AS Roma.
  3. Benfica & Boca JuniorsTitles and unforgettable nights.
  4. ScotlandDundee and Rangers.
  5. Argentina1990 World Cup finalist and 1991 Copa América champion.

His role in the agency

Claudio leads CFA's vision and relationships. He personally mentors our players, opens doors with clubs and directors around the world, and brings the perspective of someone who has lived every moment of a professional career.
